#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 1: Of the Holy Scripture

<span class="paragraph-number">4.</span> The authority of the Holy Scripture, for which it ought to be believed, and obeyed, dependeth not upon the testimony of any man, or church; but wholly upon God (who is truth itself) the author thereof: and therefore it is to be received, because it is the Word of God.[^fnref:wcf1]
