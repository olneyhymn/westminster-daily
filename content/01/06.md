#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 1: Of the Holy Scripture

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> The books commonly called Apocrypha, not being of divine inspiration, are no part of the canon of the Scripture, and therefore are of no authority in the church of God, nor to be any otherwise approved, or made use of, than other human writings.[^fnref:wcf1]

To begin with, the Jews were entrusted with the oracles of God.</p> </div> <h5>2 Peter 1:21</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For no prophecy was ever produced by the will of man, but men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
