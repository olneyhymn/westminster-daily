#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 3: Of God's Eternal Decree

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> God, from all eternity, did, by the most wise and holy counsel of his own will, freely, and unchangeably ordain whatsoever comes to pass:[^fnref:wcf1] yet so, as thereby neither is God the author of sin,[^fnref:wcf2] nor is violence offered to the will of the creatures; nor is the liberty or contingency of second causes taken away, but rather established.[^fnref:wcf3]
