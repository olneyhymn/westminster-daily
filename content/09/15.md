#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 21: Of Religious Worship, and the Sabbath Day

<span class="paragraph-number">5.</span> The reading of the Scriptures with godly fear,[^fnref:wcf1] the sound preaching[^fnref:wcf2] and conscionable hearing of the Word, in obedience unto God, with understanding, faith, and reverence,[^fnref:wcf3] singing of psalms with grace in the heart;[^fnref:wcf4] as also, the due administration and worthy receiving of the sacraments instituted by Christ, are all parts of the ordinary religious worship of God:[^fnref:wcf5] beside religious oaths,[^fnref:wcf6] vows,[^fnref:wcf7] solemn fastings,[^fnref:wcf8] and thanksgivings upon special occasions,[^fnref:wcf9] which are, in their several times and seasons, to be used in an holy and religious manner.[^fnref:wcf10]
