#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 20: Of Christian Liberty, and Liberty of Conscience

<span class="paragraph-number">2.</span> God alone is Lord of the conscience,[^fnref:wcf1] and hath left it free from the doctrines and commandments of men, which are, in anything, contrary to his Word; or beside it, if matters of faith, or worship.[^fnref:wcf2] So that, to believe such doctrines, or to obey such commands, out of conscience, is to betray true liberty of conscience:[^fnref:wcf3] and the requiring of an implicit faith, and an absolute and blind obedience, is to destroy liberty of conscience, and reason also.[^fnref:wcf4]
