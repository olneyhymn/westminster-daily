#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 21: Of Religious Worship, and the Sabbath Day

<span class="paragraph-number">2.</span> Religious worship is to be given to God, the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost; and to him alone;[^fnref:wcf1] not to angels, saints, or any other creature:[^fnref:wcf2] and, since the fall, not without a Mediator; nor in the mediation of any other but of Christ alone.[^fnref:wcf3]
