#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 8: Of Christ the Mediator

<span class="paragraph-number">7.</span> Christ, in the work of mediation, acts according to both natures, by each nature doing that which is proper to itself;[^fnref:wcf1] yet, by reason of the unity of the person, that which is proper to one nature is sometimes in Scripture attributed to the person denominated by the other nature.[^fnref:wcf2]

[^fnref:wcf1]: <div class="esv"><h5>John 10:17-18</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p><span class="woc">For this reason the Father loves me, because I lay down my life that I may take it up again.</span> <span class="woc">No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of my own accord. I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it up again. This charge I have received from my Father.”</span></p> </div> <h5>1 Peter 3:18</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit,</p> </div> <h5>Hebrews 1:3</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power. After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>

[^fnref:wcf2]: <div class="esv"><h5>Acts 20:28</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>Pay careful attention to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to care for the church of God, which he obtained with his own blood.</p> </div> <h5>Luke 1:43</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>And why is this granted to me that the mother of my Lord should come to me?</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
