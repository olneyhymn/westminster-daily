#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 9: Of Free Will

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> Man, by his fall into a state of sin, hath wholly lost all ability of will to any spiritual good accompanying salvation:[^fnref:wcf1] so as, a natural man, being altogether averse from that good,[^fnref:wcf2] and dead in sin,[^fnref:wcf3] is not able, by his own strength, to convert himself, or to prepare himself thereunto.[^fnref:wcf4]
