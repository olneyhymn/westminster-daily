#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 28: Of Baptism

<span class="paragraph-number">4.</span> Not only those that do actually profess faith in and obedience unto Christ,[^fnref:wcf1] but also the infants of one, or both, believing parents, are to be baptized.[^fnref:wcf2]
