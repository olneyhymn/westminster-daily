#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 28: Of Baptism

<span class="paragraph-number">2.</span> The outward element to be used in this sacrament is water, wherewith the party is to be baptized, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, by a minister of the gospel, lawfully called thereunto.[^fnref:wcf1]

What prevents me from being baptized?”</p> </div> <h5>Acts 8:38</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p class="same-paragraph">And he commanded the chariot to stop, and they both went down into the water, Philip and the eunuch, and he baptized him.</p> </div> <h5>Matthew 28:19</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p><span class="woc">Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,</span></p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
