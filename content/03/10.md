#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 7: Of God's Covenant with Man

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> Man, by his fall, having made himself incapable of life by that covenant, the Lord was pleased to make a second,[^fnref:wcf1] commonly called the covenant of grace; wherein he freely offereth unto sinners life and salvation by Jesus Christ; requiring of them faith in him, that they may be saved,[^fnref:wcf2] and promising to give unto all those that are ordained unto eternal life his Holy Spirit, to make them willing, and able to believe.[^fnref:wcf3]

For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed be by the law.</p> </div> <h5>Romans 3:20-21</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For by works of the law no human being will be justified in his sight, since through the law comes knowledge of sin.</p> <p>But now the righteousness of God has been manifested apart from the law, although the Law and the Prophets bear witness to it—</p> </div> <h5>Romans 8:3</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For God has done what the law, weakened by the flesh, could not do. By sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin, he condemned sin in the flesh,</p> </div> <h5>Genesis 3:15</h5> <div class="esv-text"><div class="block-indent"> <p class="line-group">I will put enmity between you and the woman,<br /> <span class="indent"></span>and between your offspring and her offspring;<br /> he shall bruise your head,<br /> <span class="indent"></span>and you shall bruise his heel.”</p> </div> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
