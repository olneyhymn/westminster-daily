#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 7: Of God's Covenant with Man

<span class="paragraph-number">2.</span> The first covenant made with man was a covenant of works,[^fnref:wcf1] wherein life was promised to Adam; and in him to his posterity,[^fnref:wcf2] upon condition of perfect and personal obedience.[^fnref:wcf3]

[^fnref:wcf1]: <div class="esv"><h5>Genesis 2:16-17</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>And the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span> God commanded the man, saying, “You may surely eat of every tree of the garden, but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die.”</p> </div> <h5>Hosea 6:7</h5> <div class="esv-text"><div class="block-indent"> <p class="line-group">But like Adam they transgressed the covenant;<br /> <span class="indent"></span>there they dealt faithlessly with me.</p> </div> </div> <h5>Galatians 3:12</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>But the law is not of faith, rather “The one who does them shall live by them.”</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>

[^fnref:wcf2]: <div class="esv"><h5>Genesis 3:22</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>Then the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span> God said, “Behold, the man has become like one of us in knowing good and evil. Now, lest he reach out his hand and take also of the tree of life and eat, and live forever—”</p> </div> <h5>Romans 10:5</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For Moses writes about the righteousness that is based on the law, that the person who does the commandments shall live by them.</p> </div> <h5>Romans 5:12-14</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>Therefore, just as sin came into the world through one man, and death through sin, and so death spread to all men because all sinned—for sin indeed was in the world before the law was given, but sin is not counted where there is no law. Yet death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those whose sinning was not like the transgression of Adam, who was a type of the one who was to come.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
