#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 8: Of Christ the Mediator

<span class="paragraph-number">4.</span> This office the Lord Jesus did most willingly undertake;[^fnref:wcf1] which that he might discharge, he was made under the law,[^fnref:wcf2] and did perfectly fulfill it;[^fnref:wcf3] endured most grievous torments immediately in his soul,[^fnref:wcf4] and most painful sufferings in his body;[^fnref:wcf5] was crucified, and died,[^fnref:wcf6] was buried, and remained under the power of death, yet saw no corruption.[^fnref:wcf7] On the third day he arose from the dead,[^fnref:wcf8] with the same body in which he suffered,[^fnref:wcf9] with which also he ascended into heaven, and there sitteth at the right hand of his Father,[^fnref:wcf10] making intercession,[^fnref:wcf11] and shall return, to judge men and angels, at the end of the world.[^fnref:wcf12]
