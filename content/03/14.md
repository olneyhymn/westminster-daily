#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 7: Of God's Covenant with Man

<span class="paragraph-number">4.</span> This covenant of grace is frequently set forth in Scripture by the name of a testament, in reference to the death of Jesus Christ the Testator, and to the everlasting inheritance, with all things belonging to it, therein bequeathed.[^fnref:wcf1]

[^fnref:wcf1]: <div class="esv"><h5>Hebrews 9:15-17</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>Therefore he is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, since a death has occurred that redeems them from the transgressions committed under the first covenant. For where a will is involved, the death of the one who made it must be established. For a will takes effect only at death, since it is not in force as long as the one who made it is alive.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
