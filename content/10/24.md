#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 25: Of the Church

<span class="paragraph-number">5.</span> The purest churches under heaven are subject both to mixture and error;[^fnref:wcf1] and some have so degenerated, as to become no churches of Christ, but synagogues of Satan.[^fnref:wcf2] Nevertheless, there shall be always a church on earth, to worship God according to his will.[^fnref:wcf3]





#### Larger Catechism

<span class="q">Q 61.</span> Are all they saved who hear the gospel, and live in the church?

<span class="q">A.</span> All that hear the gospel, and live in the visible church, are not saved; but they only who are true members of the church invisible.[^fnref:wlc1]
