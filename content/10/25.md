#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 25: Of the Church

<span class="paragraph-number">6.</span> There is no other head of the church but the Lord Jesus Christ.[^fnref:wcf1] Nor can the pope of Rome, in any sense, be head thereof.[^fnref:wcf2]
