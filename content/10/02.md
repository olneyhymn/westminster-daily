#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 22: Of Lawful Oaths and Vows

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> A lawful oath is a part of religious worship,[^fnref:wcf1] wherein, upon just occasion, the person swearing solemnly calleth God to witness what he asserteth, or promiseth, and to judge him according to the truth or falsehood of what he sweareth.[^fnref:wcf2]

[^fnref:wcf1]: <div class="esv"><h5>Deuteronomy 10:20</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>You shall fear the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span> your God. You shall serve him and hold fast to him, and by his name you shall swear.</p> </div> <h5>Isaiah 45:23</h5> <div class="esv-text"><div class="block-indent"> <p class="line-group">By myself I have sworn;<br /> <span class="indent"></span>from my mouth has gone out in righteousness<br /> <span class="indent"></span>a word that shall not return:<br /> ‘To me every knee shall bow,<br /> <span class="indent"></span>every tongue shall swear allegiance.’</p> </div> </div> <h5>Romans 14:11</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>for it is written,</p> <div class="block-indent"> <p class="line-group">“As I live, says the Lord, every knee shall bow to me,<br /> <span class="indent"></span>and every tongue shall confess to God.”</p> </div> </div> <h5>Philippians 2:10-11</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>

[^fnref:wcf2]: <div class="esv"><h5>Exodus 20:7</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>“You shall not take the name of the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span> your God in vain, for the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span> will not hold him guiltless who takes his name in vain.</p> </div> <h5>Leviticus 19:12</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>You shall not swear by my name falsely, and so profane the name of your God: I am the <span class="small-caps">Lord</span>.</p> </div> <h5>Romans 1:9</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>For God is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, that without ceasing I mention you</p> </div> <h5>2 Corinthians 1:23</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>But I call God to witness against me—it was to spare you that I refrained from coming again to Corinth.</p> </div> <h5>2 Corinthians 11:31</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, he who is blessed forever, knows that I am not lying.</p> </div> <h5>Galatians 1:20</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>(In what I am writing to you, before God, I do not lie!)</p> </div> <h5>2 Chronicles 6:22-23</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>“If a man sins against his neighbor and is made to take an oath and comes and swears his oath before your altar in this house, then hear from heaven and act and judge your servants, repaying the guilty by bringing his conduct on his own head, and vindicating the righteous by rewarding him according to his righteousness.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
