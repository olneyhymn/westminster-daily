#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 24: Of Marriage and Divorce

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> Marriage is to be between one man and one woman: neither is it lawful for any man to have more than one wife, nor for any woman to have more than one husband, at the same time.[^fnref:wcf1]
