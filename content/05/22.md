#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 14: Of Saving Faith

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> The grace of faith, whereby the elect are enabled to believe to the saving of their souls,[^fnref:wcf1] is the work of the Spirit of Christ in their hearts,[^fnref:wcf2] and is ordinarily wrought by the ministry of the Word,[^fnref:wcf3] by which also, and by the administration of the sacraments, and prayer, it is increased and strengthened.[^fnref:wcf4]
