#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 11: Of Justification

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> Christ, by his obedience and death, did fully discharge the debt of all those that are thus justified, and did make a proper, real, and full satisfaction to his Father's justice in their behalf.[^fnref:wcf1] Yet, inasmuch as he was given by the Father for them;[^fnref:wcf2] and his obedience and satisfaction accepted in their stead;[^fnref:wcf3] and both, freely, not for anything in them; their justification is only of free grace;[^fnref:wcf4] that both the exact justice and rich grace of God might be glorified in the justification of sinners.[^fnref:wcf5]
