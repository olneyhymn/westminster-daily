#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 14: Of Saving Faith

<span class="paragraph-number">2.</span> By this faith, a Christian believeth to be true whatsoever is revealed in the Word, for the authority of God himself speaking therein;[^fnref:wcf1] and acteth differently upon that which each particular passage thereof containeth; yielding obedience to the commands,[^fnref:wcf2] trembling at the threatenings,[^fnref:wcf3] and embracing the promises of God for this life, and that which is to come.[^fnref:wcf4] But the principal acts of saving faith are accepting, receiving, and resting upon Christ alone for justification, sanctification, and eternal life, by virtue of the covenant of grace.[^fnref:wcf5]
