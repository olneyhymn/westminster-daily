#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 15: Of Repentance unto Life

<span class="paragraph-number">4.</span> As there is no sin so small, but it deserves damnation;[^fnref:wcf1] so there is no sin so great, that it can bring damnation upon those who truly repent.[^fnref:wcf2]
