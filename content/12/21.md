#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 31: Of Synods and Councils

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> All synods or councils, since the Apostles' times, whether general or particular, may err; and many have erred. Therefore they are not to be made the rule of faith, or practice; but to be used as a help in both.[^fnref:wcf1]
