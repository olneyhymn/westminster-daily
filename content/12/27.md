#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 32: Of the State of Men after Death, and of the Resurrection of the Dead

<span class="paragraph-number">3.</span> The bodies of the unjust shall, by the power of Christ, be raised to dishonor: the bodies of the just, by his Spirit, unto honor; and be made conformable to his own glorious body.[^fnref:wcf1]

It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.</p> </div> <h5>Philippians 3:21</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>who will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body, by the power that enables him even to subject all things to himself.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
