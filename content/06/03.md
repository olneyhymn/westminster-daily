#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 16: Of Good Works

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> Good works are only such as God hath commanded in his holy Word,[^fnref:wcf1] and not such as, without the warrant thereof, are devised by men, out of blind zeal, or upon any pretense of good intention.[^fnref:wcf2]

Amen.  (<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div> </div>
