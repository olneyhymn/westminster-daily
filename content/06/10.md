#### Confession of Faith

##### Chapter 17: Of the Perseverance of the Saints

<span class="paragraph-number">1.</span> They, whom God hath accepted in his Beloved, effectually called, and sanctified by his Spirit, can neither totally nor finally fall away from the state of grace, but shall certainly persevere therein to the end, and be eternally saved.[^fnref:wcf1]

And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified.</p> </div> <h5>John 10:28-29</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p><span class="woc">I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.</span> <span class="woc">My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all, and no one is able to snatch them out of the Father’s hand.</span></p> </div> <h5>1 John 3:9</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>No one born of God makes a practice of sinning, for God’s seed abides in him; and he cannot keep on sinning, because he has been born of God.</p> </div> <h5>1 John 5:18</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>We know that everyone who has been born of God does not keep on sinning, but he who was born of God protects him, and the evil one does not touch him.</p> </div> <h5>1 Peter 1:5</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>who by God’s power are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.</p> </div> <h5>1 Peter 1:9</h5> <div class="esv-text"><p>obtaining the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.</p> </div> <p class="copyright-line">(<a href="http://www.esv.org" class="copyright">ESV</a>)</p> </div>
