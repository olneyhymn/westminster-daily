Abstract diapason

No tune, just the chord. For comparison against all three.

Check these by ear before we commit. The note sequences come from Hymnary's incipits, which give scale degrees but not octaves, so which octave each note falls in is inferred rather than known. For a tune people recognise, a wrong turn is worse than no tune at all. ST. ANNE is the one I trust least: its opening is conventionally a rising interval and the reconstruction here descends.
